Contact support

A reply from the person who wrote EmailEngine.

There is no ticket queue or support tier in between. A first reply can solve the problem when the email includes:

  • your EmailEngine version
  • the account type involved: Gmail API, Microsoft Graph or IMAP
  • what you expected, and what happened instead
  • the relevant log lines, with passwords, access tokens, license keys and EENGINE_SECRET removed

Email support@postalsys.com, or see the full checklist.
